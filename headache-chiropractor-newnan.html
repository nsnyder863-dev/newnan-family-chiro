Understanding Headaches

Headaches can have many different causes.

Some headache patterns may be influenced by tension in the neck and upper back, prolonged desk posture, restricted cervical movement, or muscle tightness. A careful history and examination help determine whether chiropractic care is appropriate.

Neck Tension

Tightness or discomfort in the neck may occur with certain headache patterns.

Posture Strain

Long periods at a desk, computer, or phone may contribute to neck and upper-back strain.

Recurring Symptoms

Headaches may return with stress, activity, poor sleep, or sustained positions.

Restricted Motion

Reduced or uncomfortable neck movement can accompany headache symptoms.

Our Approach

Every patient deserves an individualized evaluation.

Dr. Truong begins by discussing your symptoms, reviewing your medical history, and performing an examination. If chiropractic care is appropriate, treatment recommendations are tailored to your individual needs and goals.

Because headaches can have many causes, identifying warning signs and the likely source of symptoms is an important part of determining the most appropriate next step.

Frequently Asked Questions

Headache FAQ

Can chiropractic care help with headaches?

Some people seek chiropractic care for tension-type or neck-related headaches. After an evaluation, Dr. Truong can determine whether chiropractic care may be appropriate for your presentation.

How do I know what is causing my headache?

The location and pattern of pain alone may not identify the cause. Your history, associated symptoms, neck examination, and any warning signs help determine whether referral or conservative care is appropriate.

When should I seek emergency medical care?

Seek immediate medical attention for a sudden severe headache, headache after significant trauma, fever with a stiff neck, fainting, confusion, new weakness or numbness, vision loss, trouble speaking, or other abrupt neurologic symptoms.
