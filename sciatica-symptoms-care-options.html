Sciatica Symptoms and Conservative Care Options

Educational information from Newnan Family Chiropractic.

Sciatica is often described as pain, tingling, numbness, or burning that travels from the low back into the hip, buttock, or leg. Because not all leg pain is true sciatica, evaluation matters. Conservative care may focus on movement, mobility, posture, and reducing mechanical irritation when appropriate.

What does sciatica typically feel like?

People often use the word sciatica for pain that radiates into one leg, sometimes with tingling, burning, or numbness. Similar symptoms can occur for several reasons, including nerve irritation. A symptom description is not a diagnosis; the pattern and associated findings matter.

What to discuss during an evaluation

Tell the office whether pain travels below the knee, whether your leg feels weak, which positions make symptoms worse, and when symptoms began. An examination may help clarify whether conservative chiropractic care is suitable or whether another evaluation is indicated. Recommendations should be based on the individual, not the assumption that every leg symptom is sciatica.

When to seek urgent medical attention

New trouble controlling your bladder or bowels, numbness in the saddle area, severe or progressive leg weakness, or symptoms after major trauma warrant urgent medical assessment. For persistent symptoms without those warning signs, call the office to ask about an evaluation and options for conservative care.
