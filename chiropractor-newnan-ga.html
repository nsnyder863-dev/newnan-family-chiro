What to expect from Dr. Truong

Dr. Truong begins by asking about your symptoms, health history, daily routine, and goals. After the exam, he explains his findings and discusses care options if chiropractic care is appropriate. Explore our chiropractic services or learn more about Dr. Truong.

Frequently asked questions

Do you accept insurance?

We accept many major plans. Call the office to verify your benefits before your visit.

Do you accept VA Community Care patients?

Yes. Call the office to confirm what authorization you need before your visit.

When should I seek urgent medical care?

Seek urgent medical care for severe symptoms, major trauma, weakness, numbness, fever, loss of bowel or bladder control, chest pain, or other emergency signs.
