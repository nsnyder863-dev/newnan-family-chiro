When to Consider a Chiropractor for Back Pain

Educational information from Newnan Family Chiropractic.

Back pain is one of the most common reasons people seek chiropractic care. Some discomfort follows lifting, prolonged sitting, posture strain, work activity, or sports. Chiropractic care may be appropriate when pain affects movement or daily activity. Seek urgent care for severe trauma, weakness, numbness, fever, or bowel/bladder changes.

What might be contributing to back pain?

Back discomfort can be related to muscle strain, repetitive lifting, long periods of sitting, or a change in activity. Pain can also have causes that need medical evaluation. The location of the pain alone does not identify the cause, so be ready to discuss how it began, what movements affect it, and whether you notice symptoms in your legs.

When is a chiropractic evaluation worth considering?

If back discomfort is limiting normal movement, work, or sleep, an individual assessment can help determine whether chiropractic care may be appropriate. Dr. Truong can discuss your history, examine movement when appropriate, explain findings, and recommend further medical care if symptoms suggest another concern.

What can you do before your visit?

Keep track of when pain began, activities that aggravate it, and any prior injuries. Gentle activity within your comfort level may be reasonable for many routine episodes, but do not push through severe or worsening pain. Sudden loss of bowel or bladder control, numbness around the groin, progressive leg weakness, fever with serious illness, or pain after major trauma requires prompt medical evaluation.
