Neck Pain from Desk Work: What to Know

Educational information from Newnan Family Chiropractic.

Desk work can contribute to neck tension when the head stays forward, shoulders round, or screens sit too low. Frequent breaks, better monitor height, and movement throughout the day may help. Chiropractic evaluation may be useful when discomfort persists or limits normal activity.

How does a workday affect your neck?

Long periods in one position can contribute to stiffness and muscle fatigue. Screen height, chair support, how often you move, and whether you hold a phone between your shoulder and ear can all affect comfort. Posture is only one part of the picture, and no single desk setup works for everyone.

Simple changes to consider

Position your screen so you do not have to hold your head at an uncomfortable angle. Keep frequently used items within reach, take brief movement breaks, and vary your position during the day. If an activity increases pain, stop and consider a more comfortable alternative rather than forcing the movement.

When should neck pain be evaluated?

Neck symptoms that continue, worsen, or affect everyday tasks deserve individual evaluation. New arm weakness, significant numbness, fever with severe neck stiffness, or neck pain after significant trauma requires medical assessment.
