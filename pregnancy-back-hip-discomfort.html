Pregnancy, Back Pain, and Hip Discomfort

Educational information from Newnan Family Chiropractic.

Pregnancy changes posture, weight distribution, and joint stress. Some patients seek chiropractic care for back or hip discomfort during pregnancy. Care should be individualized, gentle, and coordinated with the patient’s healthcare team when needed, especially for high-risk pregnancies.

Why back and hip discomfort can change during pregnancy

As pregnancy progresses, changes in body weight, posture, and physical demands may affect comfort with standing, walking, and sleeping. Symptoms vary widely, so a plan that helps one patient may not suit another.

Discuss symptoms with your prenatal care team

Tell your obstetric clinician about new or changing pain, especially if your pregnancy has complications. Before any chiropractic treatment, share your stage of pregnancy and relevant health history with Dr. Truong. He can discuss whether evaluation is appropriate and coordinate with your prenatal clinician when needed. Treatment should never be presented as a way to change fetal position or guarantee a particular delivery outcome.

Warning signs that should not wait

Bleeding, fluid leakage, severe abdominal pain, fainting, fever, or other urgent pregnancy symptoms should be assessed by your prenatal care team or emergency services without delay. For routine musculoskeletal discomfort, ask the office about individualized evaluation and the precautions needed during pregnancy.
