What to Expect at Your First Chiropractic Visit

Educational information from Newnan Family Chiropractic.

A first visit usually includes a conversation about your symptoms, health history, goals, and daily activities. The chiropractor may perform an examination to better understand movement, posture, and areas of discomfort. If chiropractic care appears appropriate, recommendations are explained before care begins. You can ask questions about the proposed care before deciding how to proceed.

Plan your visit to our Newnan office

Our office is at 197 Jefferson Parkway in Newnan and serves patients across Coweta County. Read about new patient information and preparing for your first visit. Call (770) 683-9105 to ask about availability and insurance requirements.

Request an appointment

The office will contact you to confirm your request. Please share medical details during your visit or by phone, not through the general website form.

When to call the office

If symptoms are affecting your daily life, call Newnan Family Chiropractic at (770) 683-9105 or send a general message through the contact page.
